Profile & Research Interest

I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Mathematics at the University of Kansas, advised by Prof. Geng Chen. I completed my integrated BS–MS at IISER Thiruvananthapuram, India, with a major in Mathematics and a minor in Physics. My research focuses on hyperbolic PDEs, particularly L^2-stability and contraction for conservation laws, including the damped Euler and Kadomtsev–Petviashvili (KP) equations. Previously, I worked on elliptic PDEs, studying existence and regularity for (p,q)-Laplacian operators.
